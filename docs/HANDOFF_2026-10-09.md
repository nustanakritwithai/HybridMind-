## 1. Business intent — immutable unless owner changes it

Hybrid Mind is a **modern AI lifestyle media brand**, not simply a gadget shop or a website dedicated to AI glasses.

- Attract Thai readers with accurate, current AI/technology news and educational media.
- Help readers feel more informed and capable of living in the AI era.
- Build trust by explaining practical benefits, constraints, source credibility and what is not yet known.
- Recommend selectively curated products when related and useful, starting with **Smart Audio Glasses / glasses for music and phone-based voice assistants**.
- Monetize initially through the user's approved Shopee Affiliate workflow, not native e-commerce.
- The user can create up to approximately **10 content pieces per day** with AI. Scale *research and drafts*, not unchecked auto-publishing.
- The website and search discovery are central. Social channels may be secondary distribution; do not redesign around them without instruction.
- Core message: **Knowledge first, products second.** Avoid claiming buying a gadget makes anyone intrinsically smarter.
